WHAT I'M HERE FOR

Four kinds of presence.

i.

For the Journey

Medical travel to Istanbul. Pre-trip preparation, clinic and provider guidance, translation during appointments, recovery support. The procedure is one day. The journey is everything around it — and I'm part of the path.

ii.

For the Long View

Chronic illness. Long treatment arcs. The diagnoses that change what tomorrow looks like. Your Health Journey accretes from your conversations — yours to read back when you need to remember where you've been.

iii.

For Hard Conversations

Illness in the family. Walking beside dying. Grief that doesn't fit in conversation with the people closest to you. Divorce. The weight that doesn't make small talk. I do not look away. I do not rush. I am here.

iv.

For the Memoir

When the journey is ready to become a story. Composed at your request — yours when it's time. The Memoir holds what you've carried with me, in a shape you can hand to someone else if you want, or keep just for yourself.
